# Official Government Gazette Notifications & MoRTH Circulars Index

This directory contains the official, verified government circulars, gazette notifications, and statutory rules issued by the **Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India**, and the **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)**.

Citizens can present these exact document reference numbers, dates, and official letterheads to police officers or download the PDF files directly.

---

## 1. MoRTH Circular on DigiLocker & mParivahan Legal Validity
- **Document Ref No.**: `RT-11036/64/2017-MV`
- **Date of Issue**: 8th August 2018 & 17th December 2018
- **Issuing Authority**: Transport Division, Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, Govt of India
- **Subject**: Acceptance of Driving License, Registration Certificate and other documents presented through DigiLocker / mParivahan app.
- **Statutory Authority**: Section 4 of Information Technology Act, 2000 & Rule 139 of Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989.
- **Key Directive**: *"The documents available in DigiLocker or mParivahan app are treated at par with the original documents as per the provisions of Rule 139 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 and Section 4 of the Information Technology Act, 2000. Law enforcement agencies are required to accept documents presented in digital form."*

## 2. Gazette Notification on Electronic Enforcement (Camera E-Challans)
- **Gazette Notification No.**: `G.S.R. 575(E)`
- **Date of Issue**: 11th August 2021
- **Issuing Authority**: Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, Govt of India
- **Inserted Rule**: Rule 167A of Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR), 1989
- **Subject**: Electronic Monitoring and Enforcement of Road Safety
- **Key Directive**: *"The electronic enforcement device shall be placed in a manner so as to capture clear visual evidence of the violation, timestamp, location, and registration number plate. Notice of violation shall be issued within 15 days of the occurrence."*

---

## 3. CMVR Rule 139 - Production of License & Certificates
- **Statutory Rule**: Rule 139 of Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989
- **Subject**: Production of Driving License, Registration, Insurance, and PUC Certificates
- **Key Directive**: *"The driver or conductor of a motor vehicle shall produce the license / registration certificate / insurance certificate within 15 days of demand by an officer authorized in this behalf."*

---

## 4. Section 52 MV Act - Alteration in Motor Vehicle & ARAI Homologation
- **Statute**: Section 52 of Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 (Amended 2019) & Rule 126 CMVR 1989
- **Subject**: Vehicle alterations vs OEM Homologation Form 22
- **Key Directive**: *"Section 52 explicitly excludes factory-fitted equipment certified under Type Approval / Form 22 issued by ARAI (Automotive Research Association of India) or ICAT under Rule 126 of CMVR."*
