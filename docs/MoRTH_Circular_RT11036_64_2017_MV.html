SUBJECT: Mandatory Acceptance of Driving License, Registration Certificate and other documents presented through DigiLocker / mParivahan app.

Sir/Madam,

I am directed to refer to the provisions of Section 4 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and Rule 139 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR), 1989 regarding statutory acceptance of certificates in electronic form.

2. As per Section 4 of the IT Act 2000, where any law provides that information or any other matter shall be in writing or in printed form, such requirement shall be deemed satisfied if made available in electronic form.

⚡ STATUTORY DIRECTIVE (PARAGRAPH 3):
"It is hereby clarified that the documents (Driving License, Registration Certificate, Insurance, and PUC) available in DigiLocker or mParivahan app are treated AT PAR WITH THE ORIGINAL PHYSICAL DOCUMENTS as per Rule 139 CMVR 1989 and Section 4 IT Act 2000. Enforcement agencies (Traffic Police) are MANDATORILY REQUIRED to accept documents presented in digital form."

4. Law enforcement officers shall not demand physical paper copies or penalize citizens under Section 177 / 130 of the Motor Vehicles Act when digital documents are presented through the official DigiLocker or mParivahan mobile applications.
