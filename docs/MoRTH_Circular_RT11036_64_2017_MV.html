SUBJECT: Acceptance of Driving License, Registration Certificate and other documents presented through DigiLocker / mParivahan app.

Sir/Madam,

I am directed to refer to the provisions of Section 4 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and Rule 139 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR), 1989 regarding acceptance of certificates in electronic form.

2. As per the IT Act 2000, where any law provides that information or any other matter shall be in writing or in the typewritten or printed form, then, notwithstanding anything contained in such law, such requirement shall be deemed to have been satisfied if such information or matter is rendered or made available in an electronic form.

3. It is hereby clarified that the documents (Driving License, Registration Certificate, Fitness Certificate, Insurance, and PUC) available in DigiLocker or mParivahan app are treated at par with the original physical documents as per the law. Enforcement agencies (Traffic Police and Transport Department Enforcement Wing) are MANDATORILY REQUIRED to accept documents presented in digital form.

4. Law enforcement officers shall not demand physical paper copies or penalize citizens under Section 177 / 130 of the Motor Vehicles Act when digital documents are presented through the official DigiLocker or mParivahan mobile applications.
