THE MOTOR VEHICLES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019

STATUTORY PROOF LIBRARY - BATCH 2 (VIOLATIONS 11 TO 20)

Official Government Provisions, Fine Schedules & Citizen Legal Protections

11. Section 112 r/w Section 183(1)(ii) - Over-speeding (Medium / Heavy Transport Vehicle)

Statutory Fine: ₹2,000 to ₹4,000 (1st Offense) / License Seizure (Repeat Offense)

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION (SECTION 183(1)(ii)):
"Whoever drives a medium goods vehicle, medium passenger motor vehicle, heavy goods vehicle, or heavy passenger motor vehicle in contravention of speed limits under Section 112 shall be punishable with a fine of not less than two thousand rupees but which may extend to four thousand rupees."
12. Section 185, 203 & 204 - Drunk Driving & Medical Blood Test Rights

Statutory Fine: Up to ₹10,000 and/or 6 Months Jail (1st Offense) / ₹15,000 and/or 2 Years Jail (Repeat)

⚡ STATUTORY THRESHOLD & CITIZEN MEDICAL RIGHT (SECTION 204):
"Section 185 applies if alcohol detected in breath exceeds 30 mg per 100 ml of blood. Under Section 204 MV Act, a driver subjected to breath test HAS THE STATUTORY RIGHT to demand an immediate medical blood sample examination at a nearest government hospital within 2 hours of detention."
13. Section 184(c) - Handheld Mobile Phone Use While Driving

Statutory Fine: ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 (1st Offense) / ₹10,000 (Repeat Offense within 3 years)

⚡ STATUTORY EXEMPTION FOR NAVIGATION (MoRTH GUIDELINES):
"Section 184(c) prohibits holding communication devices while driving. However, as clarified by MoRTH guidelines, using a mobile phone solely for route navigation placed in a hands-free dashboard holder WITHOUT holding in hand is fully permitted."
14. Section 184 - Rash, Negligent, or Dangerous Driving

Statutory Fine: ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 and/or 1 Year Jail (1st Offense) / ₹10,000 and/or 2 Years Jail (Repeat)

⚡ STATUTORY DEFINITION (SECTION 184):
"Whoever drives a motor vehicle at a speed or in a manner which is dangerous to the public, having regard to all circumstances including nature, condition and use of the place, shall be punishable under Section 184."
15. Section 189 - Speed Racing and Trials of Speed on Public Roads

Statutory Fine: ₹5,000 and/or 3 Months Jail (1st Offense) / ₹10,000 and/or 1 Year Jail (Repeat)

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION (SECTION 189):
"Whoever without the written consent of the State Government permits or takes part in a race or trial of speed between motor vehicles in any public place shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three months or with fine of five thousand rupees."
16. Section 184 / Section 177 - Red Light Jump & Traffic Signal Violations

Statutory Fine: ₹1,000 to ₹5,000

⚡ STATUTORY CAMERA PROOF MANDATE (RULE 167A CMVR):
"Red Light Violation Detection (RLVD) camera e-challans MUST furnish clear photo/video evidence showing the vehicle front tires crossing the marked stop line while the traffic signal light was RED. Entering junction on yellow light is NOT a violation."
17. Section 184 / Section 177 - Wrong-Side / One-Way Driving

Statutory Fine: ₹1,000 to ₹5,000

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION:
"Driving a vehicle against the mandatory one-way direction sign or against the flow of traffic on divided carriageways constitutes dangerous driving under Section 184."
18. Section 194E - Blocking Free Passage of Emergency Vehicles

Statutory Fine: ₹10,000 and/or 6 Months Imprisonment

⚡ STATUTORY MANDATE (SECTION 194E):
"Whoever while driving a motor vehicle fails to draw to the side of the road, on the approach of a fire service vehicle or of an ambulance or other emergency vehicle, shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, or with a fine of ten thousand rupees."
19. Section 56 r/w Section 192 - Commercial Vehicle Fitness Certificate Lapses

Statutory Fine: ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 (1st Offense) / ₹10,000 and/or 1 Year Jail (Repeat Offense)

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION (SECTION 56):
"A transport vehicle shall not be deemed to be validly registered unless it carries a valid certificate of fitness issued by an authorized testing station under Section 56. Driving without fitness is penalized under Section 192 equivalent to an unregistered vehicle."
20. Section 66 r/w Section 192A - Operating Commercial Vehicle Without Permit

Statutory Fine: ₹10,000 and/or 6 Months Jail (1st Offense) / ₹10,000 and/or 1 Year Jail (Repeat)

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION (SECTION 192A):
"Whoever drives a transport vehicle or causes a transport vehicle to be driven in a public place without a valid permit under Section 66 shall be punishable for the first offence with a fine of ten thousand rupees and imprisonment up to six months."
