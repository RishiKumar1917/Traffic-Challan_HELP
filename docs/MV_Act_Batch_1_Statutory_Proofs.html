THE MOTOR VEHICLES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019

STATUTORY PROOF LIBRARY - BATCH 1 (VIOLATIONS 1 TO 10)

Official Government Provisions, Fine Schedules & Citizen Legal Protections

1. Section 129 r/w Section 194D - Riding Without Protective Headgear (Helmet)

Statutory Fine: ₹1,000 + 3-Month Driving License Disqualification

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION & EXEMPTION PROVISO (SECTION 129):
"Every person driving or riding a two-wheeler shall wear protective headgear conforming to standards of Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). Provided that the provisions of this section SHALL NOT APPLY to a person who is a Sikh, if he is wearing a turban while driving or riding."

Officer Authority: Sub-Inspector / Head Constable (as per State compounding notification).

2. Section 194B(1) - Driving Without Seat Belt

Statutory Fine: ₹1,000

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION (SECTION 194B(1)):
"Whoever drives a motor vehicle without wearing a safety belt or carries passengers not wearing safety belts shall be punishable with a fine of one thousand rupees."

Defense Tip: Applies to front seat occupants and rear seats fitted with factory seatbelts. Valid medical exemption certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner is exempt.

3. Section 194B(2) - Carrying Unsecured Child Under 14 Years

Statutory Fine: ₹1,000

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION (SECTION 194B(2)):
"Whoever drives a motor vehicle transporting a child below the age of fourteen years who is not secured by a safety belt or a child restraint system shall be punishable with a fine of one thousand rupees."
4. Section 128 r/w Section 194C - Triple Riding on Two-Wheeler

Statutory Fine: ₹1,000 + 3-Month Driving License Suspension

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION (SECTION 194C):
"Whoever drives a two-wheeler in contravention of Section 128 (carrying more than one passenger in addition to the driver) shall be punishable with a fine of one thousand rupees and shall be disqualified for holding a license for a period of three months."
5. Section 3 r/w Section 181 - Driving Without Valid License

Statutory Fine: ₹5,000 (1st Offense) / ₹10,000 and/or 3 Months Jail (Repeat Offense)

⚡ STATUTORY CITIZEN PROTECTION (CMVR RULE 139 & DIGILOCKER):
"Digital DL on DigiLocker / mParivahan is legally equivalent to physical original DL under Section 4 IT Act 2000 & MoRTH Circular. If physical DL is not carried at checking time, Rule 139 CMVR grants a 15-day statutory grace period to produce documents before police station."
6. Section 199A - Offences by Juveniles / Underage Driving

Statutory Fine: ₹25,000 + 3 Years Jail for Parent/Guardian + 1-Year Vehicle RC Cancellation

⚡ STATUTORY LIABILITY ON GUARDIAN (SECTION 199A):
"Where an offence under this Act has been committed by a juvenile, the guardian of such juvenile or the owner of the motor vehicle shall be deemed guilty of the offence. The registration of the motor vehicle shall be cancelled for one year, and the juvenile shall not be eligible to obtain a driving license until age 25."
7. Section 39 r/w Section 192 - Driving Unregistered Vehicle (No RC)

Statutory Fine: ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 (1st Offense) / ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 and/or 1 Year Jail (Repeat)

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION (SECTION 192):
"Whoever drives a motor vehicle or causes or allows a motor vehicle to be driven in contravention of the provisions of Section 39 (necessity for registration) shall be punishable for the first offence with a fine which shall not be less than two thousand rupees but which may extend to five thousand rupees."
8. Section 146 r/w Section 196 - Driving Uninsured Vehicle

Statutory Fine: ₹2,000 and/or 3 Months Jail (1st Offense) / ₹4,000 (Repeat Offense)

⚡ STATUTORY REQUIREMENT (SECTION 146 & 196):
"No person shall use except as a passenger a motor vehicle in a public place unless there is in force in relation to the use of the vehicle a policy of insurance complying with requirements of Chapter XI. Driving uninsured is punishable under Section 196."
9. Section 190(2) - Driving Without Pollution Under Control Certificate (PUCC)

Statutory Fine: ₹10,000 + 3-Month Driving License Disqualification

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION & NEW CAR EXEMPTION:
"Any person who drives or causes to be driven a motor vehicle in any public place violating standards prescribed for road safety, control of noise and air pollution shall be punishable under Section 190(2). Note: Brand new vehicles are exempt from PUCC testing for ONE YEAR from registration date."
10. Section 112 r/w Section 183(1)(i) - Over-speeding (Light Motor Vehicle - LMV)

Statutory Fine: ₹1,000 to ₹2,000 (1st Offense) / ₹2,000 to ₹4,000 (Repeat Offense)

⚡ STATUTORY CAMERA RADAR MANDATE (RULE 167A CMVR):
"Whoever drives a light motor vehicle in contravention of speed limits under Section 112 shall be punishable under Section 183(1)(i). Enforcement radar devices MUST be preceded by clearly visible speed limit signboards placed in advance of the checking zone."
