THE MOTOR VEHICLES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019

STATUTORY PROOF LIBRARY - BATCH 3 (VIOLATIONS 21 TO 30)

Official Government Provisions, Fine Schedules & Citizen Legal Protections

21. Section 113 r/w Section 194 - Overloading Cargo Goods Vehicle

Statutory Fine: ₹20,000 Basic + ₹2,000 per Extra Metric Tonne

⚡ STATUTORY WEIGHBRIDGE REQUIREMENT (SECTION 194):
"Whoever drives a goods vehicle carrying weight exceeding the maximum permissible gross vehicle weight specified in the registration certificate shall be punishable with a fine of twenty thousand rupees and an additional fine of two thousand rupees per metric tonne of excess load. Vehicle MUST be weighed on a certified RTO weighbridge."
22. Section 194A - Overloading Passengers in Transport Vehicles

Statutory Fine: ₹200 per Extra Passenger

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION (SECTION 194A):
"Whoever drives a transport vehicle carrying passengers in excess of the number for which seating capacity is provided in the registration certificate shall be punishable with a fine of two hundred rupees for every excess passenger."
23. Section 192A - Carrying Commercial Goods in Private Passenger Car

Statutory Fine: ₹10,000

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION (SECTION 192A):
"Using a private passenger motor car to carry commercial freight goods for hire or reward violates registration category and permit conditions under Section 66, attracting a fine of ten thousand rupees under Section 192A."
24. Section 194F - Needless Honking, Pressure Horns & Silent Zone Use

Statutory Fine: ₹1,000 (1st Offense) / ₹2,000 (Repeat Offense)

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION & SILENCE ZONES (SECTION 194F):
"Whoever drives a motor vehicle sounding a multi-toned horn, pressure horn, or needlessly honking in silence zones (designated 100 meters around hospitals, educational institutions, courts, and religious places) shall be punishable under Section 194F."
25. CMVR Rule 50 r/w Section 177 - Non-Compliant / Decorative License Plate (HSRP)

Statutory Fine: ₹5,000 to ₹10,000

⚡ STATUTORY HSRP PLATE MANDATE (RULE 50 CMVR 1989):
"License plates must strictly conform to High Security Registration Plate (HSRP) specifications, featuring chromium-based hologram laser identification numbers. Customized, regional, decorative, or illegible fonts blocking automated ANPR cameras are punishable."
26. DMVR 113(1) r/w Section 177 - Stop Line Violation at Intersections

Statutory Fine: ₹500 (1st Offense) / ₹1,500 (Repeat Offense)

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION:
"Motorists approaching a signalized road intersection MUST stop behind the painted white stop line while the traffic light is red. Creeping or stopping past the stop line is punishable under DMVR 113(1) read with Section 177."
27. Section 122 r/w Section 177 - Wrong, Obstructive, or No-Parking Parking

Statutory Fine: ₹500 to ₹1,500 (+ Authorized Towing Charges)

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION & TOWING RULES (SECTION 122):
"No person driving or in charge of a motor vehicle shall cause or permit the vehicle to remain at rest on any public road in such a position as to cause danger, obstruction or undue inconvenience to other road users. Parking in designated No-Parking zones requires clearly visible municipal signboards."
28. Section 182A(4) & Section 52 Proviso - Unauthorized Alterations & Stock Component Exemption

Statutory Fine: ₹5,000 per alteration

⚡ CRITICAL STATUTORY PROVISO TO SECTION 52(1):
"Provided that nothing in Section 52 shall apply to the alteration of, or replacement of any part of, a motor vehicle with a part, component or sub-assembly SPECIFIED BY THE MANUFACTURER, or CERTIFIED BY A TESTING AGENCY (ARAI/ICAT) under Rule 126 of CMVR 1989 (Form 22 Type Approval)."
29. Section 177A - Improper Lane Changes & Lane Discipline Failure

Statutory Fine: ₹500 to ₹2,000

⚡ STATUTORY RULES OF THE ROAD (SECTION 177A):
"Drivers must maintain lane discipline, signal before changing lanes, and yield fast lanes on expressways to overtaking traffic. Weaving erratically across marked lanes is punishable under Section 177A."
30. Section 179 - Disobeying Lawful Police Orders & Refusing Information

Statutory Fine: ₹2,000

⚡ STATUTORY PROVISION & RANK AUTHORITY (SECTION 179):
"Whoever wilfully disobeys any lawful direction given by any person or authority empowered under this Act to give such direction, or obstructs any person or authority in the discharge of any function, shall be punishable with fine extending to two thousand rupees. Note: Spot compounding requires an officer of rank Sub-Inspector (SI) or above."
