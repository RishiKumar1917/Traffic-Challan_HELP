THE MOTOR VEHICLES ACT, 1988

STATUTORY EXEMPTION FOR FACTORY OEM COMPONENTS

Rule 126 CMVR 1989 & Section 52 (Alteration in Motor Vehicle)

STATUTORY PROOF: OEM Factory Fitted Parts (Silencers, DRLs, Alloys, Crash Guards) Are Certified Under Form 22 Homologation and Cannot Be Penalized as Unauthorized Alterations.

Section 52(1) Proviso of Motor Vehicles Act, 1988:

"Provided that nothing in this section shall apply to the alteration of, or replacement of any part of, a motor vehicle with a part, component or sub-assembly specified by the manufacturer of the vehicle, or certified by a testing agency specified under Rule 126 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989."

Testing Agencies Authorized Under Rule 126 CMVR 1989:

  • Automotive Research Association of India (ARAI), Pune
  • International Centre for Automotive Technology (ICAT), Manesar
  • Central Institute of Road Transport (CIRT), Pune

LEGAL CONCLUSION FOR ON-FIELD INSPECTION:

All stock components fitted by vehicle manufacturers (e.g. Royal Enfield stock silencers, KTM factory exhausts, OEM alloy wheels, factory DRLs) undergo mandatory Type Approval and Roadworthiness Certification (Form 22). Therefore, levying fines under Section 52 for stock manufacturer components is contrary to federal motor vehicle law.
