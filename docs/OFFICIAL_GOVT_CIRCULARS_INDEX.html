Statute: Section 52(1) Proviso MV Act 1988 & Rule 126 CMVR

Gazette Notification No: G.S.R. 575(E) (CMVR Rule 167A)

Key Directive: Electronic enforcement devices must capture clear visual evidence, timestamp, location, and speed limit signages.
